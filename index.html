About

I studied Game Development at NABA in Milan, where my focus was level design and environment art. After graduating I spent a year widening the technical side — a backend track at Start2Impact covering Node.js/Express and Java, plus a lot of self-directed work on how to get real output from AI tooling.

Since March 2025 I have been at WAY, first as a generalist Unity developer on VR/MR/XR productions and, since January 2026, as project manager. I have built two complete applications on my own — one of them a freelance commission taken on independently — and I now coordinate technical teams, which means I plan the work having already done it myself.
